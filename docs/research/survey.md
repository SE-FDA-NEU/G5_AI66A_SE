## PO decisions

The survey closed at 20:00 on 17/09 with 9 responses, 8 of them from students. The decision rules were fixed before it opened and are applied here without adjustment.

| Item                                 | What the rule says                                                                                     | Decision                                                | Reason                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| BR9 and US09 warning threshold       | 70% was chosen most: 4 of 9, against 3 for 80%                                                         | Change to 70%, and give US09 a criterion at exactly 70% | The rule says change when 70% leads. It leads by a single answer, so it is a weak signal, worth checking again once people use the app                                                  |
| US07 priority                        | 3 of 9 prefer to choose the category themselves, under the 40% that would move US07 down               | Keep P1                                                 | Both respondents who had used an expense app named slow category search as a complaint, so a suggestion they can change is still worth building                                         |
| US03 time limit                      | Median 15 s over all 9 answers. Among the 8 students the two middle answers are 10 s and 15 s          | Keep 15 s                                               | The rule names no subgroup, and over all 9 the median is 15 s. Among the students the median lies between 10 s and 15 s, which does not clearly reach the 10 s that triggers the change |
| Caps for one occasion                | 3 of the 4 who set caps set them per occasion, 1 per month, none per week                              | Keep BR7 monthly; occasion caps out of scope            | An occasion has no fixed period for BR7 and BR9 to measure against. Revisit if planned events ever enter the product                                                                    |
| Scenario 1 timing                    | None of the 3 who gave a time records right after paying, so Scenario 1 should be an end-of-day review | No change needed                                        | Scenario 1 already has the student write the day's spending down each evening. US03 and US05 are both P0, and the verdict rests on 3 people                                             |
| US07 examples and default categories | Transport is not in the top three (2 of 9)                                                             | No change needed                                        | The top four are eating out 6, online shopping 5, bills and rent 4, entertainment 4. The US07 examples already use eating out and online shopping, and BR6 already lists all four       |

No story changes priority. The only rule that changes is BR9, from 80% to 70%, and US09 gains a criterion at exactly that threshold.
